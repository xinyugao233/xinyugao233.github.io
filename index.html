About me

I am a fourth-year Ph.D. candidate in the Mathematics Department at the University of Missouri, where I work under the supervision of Loukas Grafakos. My recent research also involves collaboration with Dr. Zhengchao Wan on generative models and machine learning theory. More details can be found in my CV.

I received my Bachelor's degree in Mathematics from Shandong University.

My background is in harmonic analysis and related areas of analysis, while my recent work focuses on theoretical and empirical questions in modern machine learning, particularly diffusion and generative models. I am especially interested in geometric perspectives on machine learning, memorization phenomena in generative models, and the interaction between probability, high-dimensional geometry, and learning dynamics.

Recently, I have been thinking about questions related to diffusion model memorization, noisy geometric structures in high dimensions, and how mathematical tools from analysis and geometry can help explain the behavior of modern generative AI systems.

I am also interested in:

  • Generative models and diffusion models.
  • Geometric and probabilistic perspectives on machine learning.
  • Harmonic analysis.
  • Theoretical foundations of modern AI systems.
  • Connections between rigorous mathematics and empirical machine learning.

I am currently exploring research-oriented opportunities in machine learning and generative AI, including research internships and industry research positions.
